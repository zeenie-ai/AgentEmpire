# AgentEmpire privacy policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

This policy covers AgentEmpire, the town-building game published on Google Play by
flame2talon.

## The Android app

- **It collects no personal data and shares none.** Nothing you do in the game is sent to us
  or to anyone else.
- **It has no network access.** The app does not ask for the Internet permission, so it cannot
  connect to any network. The Privacy policy button in the game menu opens this page in your
  web browser; the app itself stays offline.
- **No ads, analytics or accounts.** There are no ads, no analytics, no sign-in and no
  third-party SDKs that collect data. The game is built with the open-source Godot engine,
  which collects nothing.
- **Your town stays on your device.** The app saves your town and its settings, such as sound
  and graphics, only in its private storage on your device. They are not included in Android
  backups, and uninstalling the app removes them.

Separately from the app, Google Play shows developers anonymous statistics, such as install
counts and crash reports, which Google collects under its own privacy policy. They do not
identify you.

## The desktop version

This section is about AgentEmpire for Windows, macOS and Linux only; the Android app has no
agents. The desktop version also runs on your computer and sends nothing to us. If you choose
real agents, the game runs agent programs with your own sign-ins: Claude Code, Codex or pi.
Those programs send your tasks and the files they work on to the AI provider you signed in
with, under that provider's terms and privacy policy. Practice agents run entirely on your
computer.

## Children

AgentEmpire is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone, of any age.

## Changes

If a later version changes what the app does with data, for example by linking the phone to
the desktop version, this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new
effective date.
